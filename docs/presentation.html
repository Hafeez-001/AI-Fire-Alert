The Current Reality

The Fatal Flaws of Traditional Fire Alarms

Why legacy smoke and ionization detectors fail to prevent catastrophic fire damage:

Critical Delay
Smoke Travel Lag
Smoke takes 3 to 10 minutes to reach ceiling sensors. Flames grow exponentially within the first 3 minutes.
Blind Spots
High Ceilings & Outdoors
In warehouses, construction sites, and open yards, smoke dissipates into the air before triggering ceiling sensors.
Zero Context
Zero Visual Intel
Standard alarms emit beeps without showing first responders where the fire is, its intensity, or safe entry points.
Alarm Fatigue
Frequent False Alarms
Steam, dust, and insects trigger false alarms, causing personnel to ignore alerts or disable detectors.

Smart AI Policy

Eliminating False Alarms: The Policy Engine

A multi-stage software verification gate ensures alarms are genuine before sounding alerts:

📐
Polygon ROI Masking
Security operators draw arbitrary polygon zones per camera. Detected fire outside the polygon (e.g., stovetop or window reflection) is filtered out.
🔄
Consecutive Frame Persistence
Flames must be continuously detected across N consecutive processed frames. Light flickers, camera sensor noise, and reflections never trigger alarms.
⏱️
Intelligent Cooldown
Configurable cooldown timers throttle repeat alerts for ongoing fires, preventing notification fatigue and keeping channels clear.
